FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does an app or website cost?+
It depends on what it needs to do. A simple business website and a booking app with logins and payments are very different jobs. Send a short WhatsApp message about your business and I'll reply with a free, fixed quote within a day.
How long does it take?+
You get the timeline with your quote, before anything starts, and a working demo on your phone every week.
Can you publish my app on the Play Store and App Store?+
Yes. I handle the store listing, testing and release on both Google Play and the Apple App Store.
I already have a website. Can you improve it?+
Yes. I can speed it up, fix how it shows on phones, set it up for Google search, or rebuild it if that's cheaper in the long run.
Who owns the app and the code?+
You do. You get the code, the store accounts and the hosting in your name.
Do you only work with clients in Trichy?+
No. I'm based in Tiruchirappalli and happy to meet local clients in person, and I work remotely with clients across India and abroad.
What happens after launch?+
I can keep your app or website running: updates, fixes, hosting, backups and new features when you need them.
