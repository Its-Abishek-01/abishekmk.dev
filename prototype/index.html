    How it fits together

    Follow one request through the Massclick platform. Scroll, or tap any dot.

    01 / 08

    It starts on a phone.

    The Flutter app you just saw taken apart, from its first commit to v2.1.1.

    02 / 08

    Or in a browser.

    The React site renders its business-page hero on the server, so it appears before any JavaScript loads on a budget phone.

    03 / 08

    Through the edge.

    nginx balances two API replicas. I traced exactly why every deploy used to drop the whole site.

    04 / 08

    Into the engine.

    The Express API: search with category intent and distance ranking, auth policies, leads, payments and certificates.

    05 / 08

    Down to the data.

    MongoDB on its own server after a ~90 s cutover, Redis in front, and 36,187 S3 files moved to clean keys.

    06 / 08

    Back out, instantly.

    Leads are saved first. Then WhatsApp, push and live socket alerts go out independently, so a failed alert never loses a lead.

    07 / 08

    Money moves.

    PhonePe checkout, Pay2All bill fetch and recharge, and a QR-verified certificate issued the moment a business pays.

    08 / 08

    Something is always watching.

    Prometheus and Grafana alerts. When Googlebot's junk crawl tripped them, I fixed the cause and rewrote the rule.
