Freelance Full-Stack Engineer — VandiZone (direct client)

Ongoing

A marketplace for hiring JCBs, cranes and goods trucks · sole developer of all four surfaces: the customer app, the captain and fleet-owner app, the office console and the public website, on Firebase with Cloud Functions

  • Dispatch engine: a job is offered only to captains who are on duty, verified, driving the right model, within range of the site, and whose machine genuinely fits the load — payload in tons, body type, hazardous certification, trailer length, and a crane's lift weight at the height asked. Immediate bookings search at once, scheduled ones begin shortly before the start time, and an unaccepted booking times out at no charge. Office staff can hand-pick a captain, with the booking held while they choose.
  • Proof of work: every job runs as gated stages that must be proved before the next opens — GPS arrival, 4-digit codes that appear only in the customer's app and never on the captain's phone, and compulsory completion photos. Truck jobs add loaded weight, a weighbridge slip, an e-way bill photo and number, a platform-issued seal number, and a separate code from the receiver at delivery.
  • Pricing, hourly: a rate card with a one-hour minimum then quarter-hour blocks, flat charges for bringing a machine and taking it home, distance beyond the covered round trip, a full-day discount, and per-model minimum floors. The customer sees an estimate at booking; the final bill is recomputed from the real time worked and the machine's real journey.
  • Pricing, reverse auction: for trucks the customer names the unit and the rate against a suggested market figure with a hard floor, and captains either accept or counter. Counter-offers are blind to each other and presented cheapest-first; the customer can raise their rate, which re-notifies everyone holding the offer.
  • Settlement: halt charges the customer accepts or rejects, shortage deductions the captain or owner can dispute, and mid-job cash advances netted off the bill — with the bill locked while any of them is undecided, and a queue in the console for the ones needing a human decision.
  • Money and tax: Razorpay payments verified server-side before a booking reads as paid, a customer wallet, cash collection, payment links for customers without the app, refunds credited to the wallet, commission and GST taken from each fare, owner payouts with negative-balance recovery on cash jobs, and reviewed bank withdrawals. Tax invoices are issued per booking with CGST and SGST split in-state or a single IGST line otherwise, and are immutable — a wrong invoice is cancelled and reissued.
  • Policy as data: cancellation tiers are computed on the pre-tax price and frozen onto each booking at confirmation, so changing the rate card later can never rewrite what an old booking agreed to.
  • Supply side: verification queues for captains, owners and machines with reasons the applicant sees, edit-with-notification, and type changes that keep a machine's verification; expiring insurance or fitness quietly removes a machine from dispatch after reminders. Owners register machines with capability figures, invite captains, set a duty chart of who may drive what, and watch a live fleet board of who is on a job, idle or off duty.
  • Live operations: captain location that keeps reporting through a closed app and a locked screen, a "no signal" flag when it stops, an office live map with route replay, shareable public tracking links stripped of phone numbers and amounts, and in-app chat and calling scoped to the job and read-only afterwards.
  • Reliability on the driver's phone: job offers ring on the alarm channel so Silent and Vibrate cannot mute them, and a built-in diagnostic fires a test offer and then audits alarm volume, Do Not Disturb, the notification channel, battery saver, background restriction, location permission and the push connection, each with a one-tap fix.
  • Office console and website: around twenty modules — bookings with settlement, work pipeline, photos, route trail and chat; phone bookings with a live bill preview; customers, verification, refunds, withdrawals, invoices, GST and revenue reports, pricing and notifications — plus a public site with instant quotes, captain sign-up, a load board with an approval queue, and a public page for checking that a vehicle, captain or booking code is genuine. Categories, rates, dispatch tuning, forced updates and maintenance mode all change from the console with no app release.
